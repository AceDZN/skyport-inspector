## Reusability for the50-level campaign

The hero module is suitable across courtyards, conveyors, workshops, circuit relays and vertical rooms. It is compact, neutral and brand-free, with useful real edge/handle/hatch detail. Shared geometry/material clones avoid duplicating mesh buffers; five material primitives mean five draws per uninstanced module, so dense conveyor scenes should use shared batches/instancing per material. Size/owner/shape plaques can vary independently of mesh identity without revealing the correct physical goal by color.

The optional porter can remain a scripted facility guide or recorded actor. It has a real65-joint rig and useful walk/idle clips. For multiple independent characters, clone skeletons independently and use separate mixers; a generic deep Object3D clone is not sufficient for independent skeletal playback. Its brass/teal style fits a friendly workshop role, although it is less neutral than the ceramic cargo.

The procedural ceramic floor/panel, graphite frame, pad and folding-bridge kit can support different mechanical room families with consistent materials. The new campaign's variety must come from distinct spatial layouts and functioning mechanics, rather than merely moving the same two panels or changing prop colors. No extra models are necessary to begin those families. The portal A/B labels and shape markers, module plaques and readable local Hebrew/Latin fonts remain useful.
