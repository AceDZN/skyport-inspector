A defining relative clause identifies a particular person or object among alternatives; it belongs next to the noun it describes. Distinguish the role inside the relative clause from the noun phrase's role in the main clause. Subject and object roles can reverse who affects whom. Extra-information clauses have different punctuation and cannot be used as arbitrary identification requirements. Future puzzles should offer multiple plausible physical objects and require the relationship, rather than merely repeating a destination as the last word.
