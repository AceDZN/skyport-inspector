## Exact implemented contract

`src/core/main.ts` forwards active-game physical event names to `audio.cue()`, mapping `blocked` and `wrong-exit` to `invalid`. Most world/log/report names have no buffer and are intentionally silent. Future source now reserves `complete` for an accepted result and calls `pauseWithCompletion()`; this new lifecycle now passes Root’s targeted strict check and six fake-WebAudio unit contracts, while actual browser playback remains pending. See `completion-source-change/API.md` and Root’s `MAIN_INTEGRATION.json`. A previous build that still calls ordinary pause immediately after complete can cut the **2.600 s** figure and freeze its remaining tail; do not count a cue counter as full completion playback.

The runtime list remains 29 local buffers: 26 action files plus synchronized score, connection and indoor-filtered environment loops. One AudioContext/three continuous loops are reused on Start, room changes, Retry and Restart. Flight air is a separate generated source, only while airborne speed exceeds 6; main passes zero whenever grounded. Its fade/disconnect is delayed, so allow 240 ms after a grounded/paused update before requiring `airActive:false`.

Current main uses three chapter bands: rooms1–17 →1,18–34 →2,35–50 →3, at intensity0. Module capacity1–5 is not five implemented campaign tracks. A placed pair reveals the existing counter-line; current core does not call a bridge-specific layer or intensity changes. Current UI exposes **master** volume and a sound test. Music/SFX/voice remain independent persisted module gains, but do not claim four current UI sliders or a spoken-language assessment. No campaign speech playback is implemented. Sound is supplementary to visible mechanics/text.

## Common lifecycle checks on the final frozen build

Capture before/after `render_game_to_text` (audio plus physical world/transport state), UI action, bundle hash and actual output evidence. Do not inject bodies/portals/completions/storage or call cue APIs to substitute for real events. Use an isolated legitimately earned profile for replay and label it as replay; fresh campaign evidence remains separate.

- [ ] **Cold load/autoplay:** before a real Start/Continue gesture context is not-started. Real loading screen/menu stays silent and responsive. After fetch/decode,29 buffers/3 loops/loadErrors[]; no duplicate contexts, requests or music phase restart on the next room.
- [ ] **Loading failure/retry:** distinguish `retry-load` (engine/assets initialization) from audio fetch diagnostics, room Retry and Restart. Audio loading catches/records failed files but current `start()` does not retry missing buffers. Verify clear diagnostics/graceful silence, do not claim a fresh decode retry that is not implemented. Cold-start early cues before buffers load may be absent; verify important first physical feedback after readiness.
- [ ] **Master mute:** change the actual UI slider to0 while running, use real portal/cargo events and wait out ramp; sampled destination output is zero although cue/event counters may increase. Restore.8, reload/Continue and verify persisted value. Repeat during result completion and settings preview.
- [ ] **Other stored gains:** where an approved module/lifecycle probe is used, independently vary music/SFX/voice and restore originals. In public core only master has a slider; no unsourced UI claim. Voice bus has no current recordings.
- [ ] **Pause/help/journal:** Esc, M and N/LOG stop physics/input, clear air, fade normal room audio, then context suspends after about130ms. Reading reports must produce no extra machine/correctness sound. Esc/Return resumes existing context/loops. Resize while paused redraws without resuming physics/audio or duplicating sources.
- [ ] **Settings sound/Back:** explicit sound test temporarily resumes audio and plays portalA while world remains paused; after1s it re-pauses. Press Back/Home/Resume immediately during loading or preview and wait beyond1s: ticket cancellation must not later resume or pause the wrong screen. Resize does not restart preview. Always check running mute output, not a silent suspended context alone.
- [ ] **Focus:** actual native blur/hidden/pointer-lock loss pauses play. Focus alone need not auto-resume; use ordinary Resume. Do not count headless tab creation as OS blur evidence.
- [ ] **Retry from pause:** resume occurs **before** `world.respawn()`, so its cue survives asynchronous context resume; exactly one respawn cue, old air gone, three loops preserved. R during play remains running. Restart/Continue/new room keeps one score phase, correct chapter and resets connection layer until real placement.
- [ ] **Accepted result:** physics freezes, room music/air/old contact/motor tails stop, one complete figure finishes for2.6s, then context suspends. During this intentional phase expect `paused:true`, `completionActive:true`, context running; after end completionActive false. This differs from ordinary pause.
- [ ] **Result cancellation:** Next/Restart/Home/levels during the figure must stop it immediately; no tail or old onended callback may later sound/suspend the new room. Repeat with muted master and changed saved music/SFX. Rejected stale save generation goes Home with warning and no victory cue.
- [ ] **Final50:** genuinely accepted freight/alternate result plays once; no invented room51, terminal result menu/Replay/Continue retain real completed50/frontier50 data. No stage-tone or result hook may fabricate progression. Verify through ordinary UI on final integration, not native level override.

## Recurring machinery, collision and headroom checks

- [ ] **Quiet control:** stand idle beside an untouched cube/pad/deck for15s. Count producer events by device/body, accepted cues and output separately. Resting cargo should not continuously contact/press; a conveyor should not continuously clutch.
- [ ] **Continuous motor:** observe one full native motion leg plus dwell; ordinary start/stop only, not alternating motor/brake from tiny render remainders. Current main uses a fixed120Hz accumulator; Systems uses a dt-relative motion threshold. The historical C11 55motor/54brake observation is a repaired-source regression target, not an accepted all-50 baseline.
- [ ] **Optical interruption:** deliberate genuine power loss during loaded travel gives one physical brake; sustained dark pause is quiet, restoring resumes once without phase jump. Parked mode retains position while current-supply door closes; no spurious arrival at an unvisited stop.
- [ ] **Conveyor loop:** actual ON/OFF/reverse and loose cargo delivery, then at least two ordinary player crossings/boarding off a stationary powered belt. Root’s C45 stationary KCC query candidate is a physics repair under separate verification; audio should neither mask pinning nor claim it fixed. At steady ON expect clutch quiet; reversal switch remains distinct from repeated contacts.
- [ ] **Contact density:** real cube landing can give drop+contact+pad/door and another body can cross a portal; keep these causal events. Reject constant contact at rest/seams, not legitimate independent impacts. Inspect raw world events because mixer guards/cap can hide upstream floods.
- [ ] **Multiple supplies/timers:** two simultaneous plates/shutters/receivers and repeated real timer cycles may overlap. Check invalid/completion remain intelligible; eight-shot cap remains bounded. Source cap/headroom alone does not prove the mix is pleasant.
- [ ] **Air:** real2/16/17/40/46/50 fling, high-speed fall and landing. No air on normal walking, stationary lift/carrier, grounded conveyor ride, pause or final result. One reused air source, smooth release/disconnect. Do not require held-cube fling where recipe uses walking return.
- [ ] **Measure + listen separately:** passive destination sample peaks/RMS at default and max controls, mute0, no decode/page errors or nonfinite samples. Record sample window and gain; avoid continuous true-peak/loudness claims from sparse samples. Audition actual final output for fatigue, loop seams, cue contrast and clipping. Mark performer/date/device; never convert an analyser/context state or silent Playwright video into a human listening claim.
