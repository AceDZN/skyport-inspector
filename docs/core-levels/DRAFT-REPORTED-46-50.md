# Reported instructions 46–50 · design for review

**Source-only proposals; no fixtures, targets, registration, build or completion claim.** Read the complete cached [G9 reported-speech source](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-9/chapter-11/reported-speech-reference) before these original examples. This stage preserves the message, adjusts speaker/listener pronouns and possessives, and recognises reported questions/requests. It does **not** demand tense backshift. Both *said that it is...* and the unchanged-tense model prescribed by this source are retained; no past-perfect conversion is required. Optional *that* after *say/tell* does not create a meaning-changing contrast.

No animated new character is needed. Clearly identified facility stations can issue short textual instructions. Direct/report pairs below are authored procedures, not fabricated machine-event history. If a message asserts a current machine state, that assertion must come from its real sensor; otherwise phrase it as an instruction or circuit rule. A physical plaque binds each station's receiver/button/hatch to actual visible wiring. Do not resolve *its/their/my* through a hidden cargo-owner tag or an ambiguous pronoun chain.

Both source messages and physical alternatives exist in each fixed fixture. A contrast changes the identified speaker, addressee-owned supply or requested operation; changing quotation marks alone never changes the correct world outcome. No relay quiz or “report accepted” flag opens an otherwise solved door.

## 46 · Its Receiver — reported source selects a real supply

**Source at LOADING:** “My receiver powers my route.”

**Base:** “LOADING says that its route is powered by its receiver. Keep that receiver lit and take the route.”

**Same-fixture contrast:** “GALLERY says that its route is powered by its receiver. Keep that receiver lit and take the route.”

**Physical assembly/chain:** An enclosed fork has LOADING's receiver-powered freight carrier and GALLERY's independently receiver-powered vertical lift. One optical pair can light either receiver. Read the station heading → map *its* to that station's actual supply → route the real beam → wait for stopped carriage/dock → reach the corresponding destination. The named stations have different physical routes, not generic doors distinguished only by a word.

**Wrong/recovery/equivalent:** Lighting the other supply powers its actual machine and permits a wrong-bay visit without the requested unlock. Return on that machine or through the catch stairs, then correct the link. A geometrically equivalent beam route remains valid. Verify source ownership against actual circuits and both current stopped/powered outcomes. No tense change or exact cargo identity is evaluated.

## 47 · I and Its — retain the actual recipient

**Source at ENTRY, addressed to the operator:** “Keep my access button pressed while you take the cargo through my hatch.”

**Base report:** “ENTRY told me that I must keep its access button pressed while I take the cargo through its hatch.”

**Same-fixture contrast:** “FREIGHT told me that I must keep its access button pressed while I take the cargo through its hatch.”

**Physical assembly/chain:** A two-room transfer workshop has a pressure-maintained ENTRY walking hatch and a separate FREIGHT cargo hatch leading to a lower delivery bay. The player is the clearly established original listener/operator: *you* in the source becomes *I* in the operator's report; *my* becomes the source station's *its*. Retrieve two interchangeable cubes through separate portal-access alcoves, maintain the selected access button with one, and deliver the other through that real hatch to its receiving bay. Both supplies and deliveries exist unchanged.

**Wrong/recovery/equivalent:** Removing the support too early closes the corresponding actual hatch; an incorrect source mapping opens the other route. Replace cargo and take a safe return loop to correct it. Body/portal pressure alternatives are accepted if they genuinely work. Verify the source/listener headings, maintained access, real cargo aperture fit and both delivered destinations. The modal preserves an operational obligation; it does not turn a report into a new permission flag.

## 48 · Asked If — inspect, then act on the actual answer

**Base:** “CONTROL asked if the freight lift is at LOADING. If it is not, call it and wait. Board it at LOADING and use the freight gallery.”

**Same-fixture contrast:** “CONTROL asked if the inspection carrier is at SERVICE. If it is not, call it and wait. Use its docked deck to cross the service walkway.”

**Physical assembly/chain:** An enclosed transport rotunda has a loading lift, an independently docked service carrier and solid observation landings. Read the reported question with statement order → inspect the actual selected machine/dock → use its real E call if absent → wait for stopped alignment → board/bridge the requested route. “Asked if” is a report, not proof that the answer is yes. The direct source question can be shown as “Is the freight lift at LOADING?” without asking the player to type or click a conversion.

**Wrong/recovery/equivalent:** A past dock message or selected target cannot supply support over an empty shaft. A wrong call moves the actual other machine; correct it, wait another cycle or use the catch return. Both dock circuits stay physical and current. Verify no command-time “arrival,” actual query wording, missed boarding, safe fall and repeat call. This is recognition and action on a reported question, not a grammatical answer gate.

## 49 · Here and There — keep the message's physical reference

**Direct source at LOADING:** “Leave the cube here and keep my receiver lit.”

**Base report copied onto the GALLERY board:** “LOADING told the operator to leave the cube there and keep its receiver lit. The copy refers to LOADING.”

**Same-fixture contrast on the same GALLERY board:** “GALLERY told the operator to leave the cube here and keep its receiver lit. This instruction refers to GALLERY.”

**Physical assembly/chain:** A paired observation vault has a real pressure/receiver circuit at LOADING and another at GALLERY, joined by a portal-access return loop. Both original/copy headings are visible at their actual physical boards. Reach/read the GALLERY copy → retain the identified source location → carry ordinary cargo to its real pressure plate → maintain that station's receiver and use its actual lift or return carrier. *Here* does not automatically become *there*: the reference changes only when the report moves to a different location. The source labels remove ambiguity rather than guessing from camera direction.

**Wrong/recovery/equivalent:** Dropping at the nearby but wrong station powers its different route; return/retrieve and correct the physical allocation. Both pads accept any cargo. The original sentence is an instruction, not a claim that someone already left a cube. Verify actual board positions and source/copy clarity, both supplies and their distinct route consequences. A report visible through a portal keeps the board's stated location frame; no invisible player-position grammar rule is added.

## 50 · Their Transfer — mediate a concise multi-system plan

**Source group LOADING + FREIGHT:** “Keep our receiver lit. Deliver a cube to our STORE. Use our transfer route.”

**Base report:** “LOADING and FREIGHT told the operator to keep their receiver lit, deliver a cube to their STORE and use their transfer route.”

**Same-fixture contrast:** “GALLERY and ARCHIVE told the operator to keep their receiver lit, deliver a cube to their STORE and use their transfer route.”

**Physical assembly/chain:** The final enclosed facility links two visibly different multi-stage branches. LOADING/FREIGHT's supply operates a horizontal cargo carrier followed by a latched freight passage and a final pressure/beam lift. GALLERY/ARCHIVE's supply operates a vertical retrieval route and a separate returning inspection walkway. Each station group has a plainly identified shared receiver and receiving plate tied to its actual circuit; every listed object and both branches are fixed before mission choice. No new mechanic is introduced at50.

Read the named source group → preserve plural *their* through each short instruction → establish actual optical power → retrieve/transport ordinary cargo → maintain its real STORE pressure → physically cross the group's connected route. Safe landings and previously proven non-reset returns separate stages. The source message is a plan; a genuine event journal may later describe completed portions, but it never fabricates a delivery or marks a stage complete because its text was read.

**Wrong/recovery/equivalent:** A wrong group's receiver or STORE powers the other actual branch, not the requested transfer. Trace the actual supply, restore it and reuse cargo through the safe return. All physically equivalent routes/assignments remain accepted. No source-owner cargo ID, minimum stage-use count or “heard all instructions” gate is added. The last room's actual completion must produce a real final result, retain the earned frontier and survive reload/replay without relying on a nonexistent next level.

## Evidence and source boundary

The verified source uses *say* without a direct personal recipient and *tell* with one, allows optional *that*, changes pronouns to match actual speaker/listener, recognises *ask if* and statement order, and reports instructions with *tell someone to*. It explicitly does not require backshift at this curriculum scope. The examples above do not claim that backshift is generally wrong English. These are situated reading/mediation tasks, not free-writing assessment or whole-unit mastery. Optional Hebrew pauses may explain who speaks, who receives the instruction and what each possessive refers to.

Every branch still requires an inspected original construction target, root-reviewed explicit fixture/circuits independent of message text, source/recipient-reference audit, native authored-spawn success/wrong/recovery and a frozen ordinary-input same-fixture contrast. Actual machine/cargo/optical contact and saved progression remain authoritative. The final fresh1→50 sequence, all-earned save and audiovisual/control acceptance are still mandatory; these twenty planning rows are not twenty implementations or completions.
