## Authority and assessment

`World` is the single authoritative case model. `resolveJourney` applies updates only when journey ID, date, and destination all match. `expectedAction` checks today, holder, confirmation and any current check-in requirement. `evaluate` checks stamp and, for BOARD, route time/gate. HOLD sends the traveler to the correction queue; gate/time fields are irrelevant to a HOLD. Unknown facts require clarification.

Comprehension credit requires the correct action and exactly two available line IDs matching a reviewed pair. Selecting every line, fabricated lines, duplicates or unrelated phrases does not earn credit. A valid stamp without a supported pair can advance the story with no comprehension credit. This finite rubric measures observed source-use on these cases; it does not assess every possible valid English explanation. Evidence lines are never automatically highlighted as correct.

The engine supplies `correct` and `comprehension` independently. The integrated game is responsible for logging first attempts, hints/translation, retries, recall, and transfer, and for identifying assistance when reporting outcomes. Merely selecting two correct lines after feedback must not be reported as an unaided first-attempt demonstration.

## Coverage limits

G7 and G8 chapter-11 sentence/reference strands are supporting gameplay only: G7 chooses one precise information question; G8 tracks unambiguous pronouns. The exact supplied supporting snapshots are chapter 9 (`tickets-and-character-choices`, `tracking-reference-and-connections`); this must not be advertised as completion of a chapter-11 rule module. G9 directly implements the supplied chapter-11 reported-speech module’s identity/reference and reported-request reading.

The game does not teach/assess the full tense system, free verb-form production, extended prose, all questions, free writing, literature analysis, graphs, pronunciation, speaking, listening proficiency, or retained mastery. Optional Hebrew translations are assistance. No conclusion about learning gains follows from these unit tests or automated play.

## Verification

Semantic suite: `node --import tsx --test tests/semantics.test.ts`. It checks all 12 authored launch/replay cases, wrong stamps, unsupported evidence, all three meaning-changing pairs, missing information, update scope, preserved fields, current versus past check-in, report identities/dates, finite reasoning and an ambiguous “lost ... yesterday” regression. Browser verification is recorded separately in PLAYTEST.md; these unit tests do not prove controls, rendering, audio quality or enjoyment.
