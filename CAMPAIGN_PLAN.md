## Proposed structure (review before broad implementation)

1. Thresholds1–10: wall-to-wall placement, distinct split/L/Ucourtyards, glass barriers, carrying, return paths and bridge pads. Teach physical verbs and spatial/sequence English by changing actual target/location/order.
2. Circuit Garden11–20: floor switches, latching shutters, AND/ORmulti-pad bridges, lifts, permissions/negation and scoped machine updates. Distinct room graphs rather than cosmetic variants.
3. Echo Workshop21–30: conveyors/material flow and actual robot carry→drop→beside replays, ownership/reference/event/background and reported instructions. Different cargo and routing plans, not renamed same room.
4. Gravity Forge31–40: vertical shafts, floor-to-wall velocity rotation, safe receivers/landing zones, height/launch sequencing and current maintenance/conditions. Preserve facing/speed across real planes.
5. Rift Relay41–50: mixed mechanic transfer through asymmetric multi-space chambers, two-object orchestration, relinking, conveyors/lifts/shutters/launches and practical-source constraints. Finale combines taught verbs without introducing an untested mechanic.

Each chapter uses teach→guided→independent→changed-context transfer rhythm; difficulty comes from spatial planning and English constraints, not reading speed.
