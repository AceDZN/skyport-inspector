`evaluateRoom` consumes authoritative world actions. Physical completion needs an actual crate on a pad, a bridge crossing and arrival at the exit. Language-goal credit additionally requires the specified crate, side and route. Portal traversal is recorded separately; the integration must make its core transport mechanics physically necessary rather than treating an arbitrary counter as an English answer. Momentum credit requires actual launch use only where the mission explicitly requires it.

No phrase-pair selection, free-writing claim, tense-name quiz, whole-chapter completion, speaking/listening proficiency or retained-mastery claim. Current bridge safety must be stated as a world fact; a past-simple event does not imply current damage.
