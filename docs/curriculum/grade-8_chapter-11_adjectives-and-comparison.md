Comparisons must specify the relevant property and comparison set. A comparative relates two items; a superlative selects an extreme within a defined group. Equality in one property does not establish identity in other properties. Difficulty, height, distance and speed require actual authored measurements or visibly reliable relationships. Do not describe a route as shorter merely because its sign says so. Original game examples should make the full relation select a physical object or route. Mechanical success alone does not prove grammar production or retention.
