# Mockup versus real gameplay

Built-in imagegen target was generated and inspected before implementation. Exact prompt and original output are in docs/visual-targets. It is a design mockup, not a game screenshot.

Real desk and readable comparison captures are docs/playtest/desk.png and compare.png. Art owner additionally captured native Chrome gameplay and inspection in docs/art.

The implementation retains the seated fixed camera, navy desk/enamel/brass/ivory palette, lamp, bell, rubber stamp, two physical paper slots, waiting visitor and airship dock. All document text comes from actual fixtures and has a large LTR inspection face. Paper dragging uses a world raycast and snaps at release; arm/confirm creates a single stamp consequence.

First capture exposed a dark metallic traveler and excessive airship crop. Front lighting, scene environment reflections, model scale/distance and airship placement were adjusted. Readable inspection text is crisp, with selectable evidence marked neutrally rather than automatically revealing correctness. The world occupies the canvas; brief controls do not replace the 3D desk.

Intentional differences: a commissioned owned brass/teal automaton with a real rig replaces the mockup human; no close foreground hand, crowd or facial rig is claimed. The original procedural airship/dock is simpler than the cinematic target. Scene depth and PBR models are real, while exact English paper faces and readable HTML inspection are generated at runtime for legibility. The header and route/stamp strip are functional controls.

Final gameplay captures and independent art review are kept alongside first-review evidence so the quality changes are reviewable. Software WebGL rendering disables shadows and caps pixel ratio for responsive fallback; hardware rendering uses capped shadows/environment lighting. No claim of identical photorealism is made.

Final pass: reduced total light/exposure and used an unlit printed-paper top material, so document ink retains its exact contrast while edges remain 3D. Final desk.png/scoped-update.png/board-stamp.png were opened and visually inspected by lead. Visible BOARD ink is applied to a tangible paper; selected gate/time are shown in the imprint. Roughly68 seconds of continuous real input is in source-loop.mp4; 26 states and zero page errors are in loop-states.json. Independent short-landscape close-control defect was fixed and retested. Some exterior surfaces remain intentionally simple and the background airship is still cropped; photorealistic equivalence is not claimed.
