# Early enclosed chamber targets3–5

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, then visually inspected before authoring the corresponding geometry. These are design targets, not gameplay screenshots, playtest wins or evidence that3–5 are implemented. Exact initial/edit prompts are alongside the selected images. `manifest.json` records bytes, dimensions and hashes. No Tripo model/rig job or credit spend occurred.

- **03 / Behind the Window:** accepted final `chamber-03-target.png`. Two neutral red buttons are clearly on opposite sides of the same clear longitudinal glass divider; one original diamond cube is on the player side. Same-height parallel bays, north vestibule and real door circuit channels read. Candidate cyan oval appeared ambiguous on/over the glass, so a targeted edit removed it; the remaining amber portal is on the remote ceramic wall. The off-camera second portal must be on an actual eligible white wall. Runtime uses the full English module (including north destination) and small NORTH/SOUTH direction signs, not an image-inferred goal. South exit is deliberately off camera.
- **04 / Trade Places:** accepted `chamber-04-target.png`. Depressed button edge anchors the eye-height viewpoint, raised opaque access shutter reveals one cube and a valid remote portal wall, while the separate dark east exit branch/door is visible. The L junction, opaque sight-line dependency, mechanical retraction housing and contrasting non-portalable exit corridor are useful construction references. Actual collision/circuit data must establish the tradeoff; a generated open door is not proof.
- **05 / Two Together:** accepted edited `chamber-05-target.png`. Exactly two identical diamond cubes, a glass-enclosed low retrieval cell, separate high retrieval gallery, two red buttons at different elevations and one closed exit with two neutral lamps are countable. The initial candidate painted a large mission onto the wall; a targeted edit restored ceramic and moved the text into a restrained HUD. Actual game art reuses the original local module/device rather than importing the rendered concept objects.

All three outputs retain richer wear/reflections than the present real-time kit. Use their deliberate construction, sight lines, functional compartmenting and scale; do not claim photorealistic parity. Enclosures, surfaces, doorways and routes are authored from explicit coordinates independent of the selected mission. Publication waits for normal-input base/contrast/recovery routes, actual first-person target comparisons and physics checks.
