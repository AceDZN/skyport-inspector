## Remaining visual limits and verification

- The generated target has more detailed wear, reflection and local contrast than this real-time material kit. The actual game has restrained procedural base/roughness/normal treatment; it is not a scanned-material or AAA rendering claim.
- Portal outlines are clean cyan/amber tubes with coherent spill. The target's complex energized edge treatment is richer. Linked-view perspective, bounded recursion and exit clipping require continued normal-input camera/transition review; appearance alone cannot certify them.
- Source/connected-space cube copies preserve actual rigid orientation and use local clipping planes. Their implementation exists; a clear partial-carry sequence must still be inspected in actual normal-input footage before calling that interaction visually verified.
- Viewmodel and wall labels no longer dominate the play view. Contextual hints, help/pause and English mission are lead-owned UI. Debug/harness labels are absent from the delivered UI.
- Housing, source clipping and material cleanup must remain correct on room change/restart and across actual GPU/software paths. Software uses deliberately cheaper light/view updates and is not visually identical to hardware.

The final evidence should include actual Chamber1 retrieval/carry/button/door footage and Chamber2 drop/fling/landing footage, with intermediate screenshots and errors. Successful unit probes and seeded access shots remain separate from played completion. The lead has reported independent normal-input passage through both core gates; that assertion should be linked to the lead/QA evidence rather than attributed to the art inspection captures.
