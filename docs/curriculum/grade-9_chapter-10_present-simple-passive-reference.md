The present simple passive combines the appropriate form of be with a past participle. The grammatical subject is affected by the action; a by-phrase can identify the actor, and the actor may be omitted. An omitted actor cannot be inferred from the passive sentence alone. In game circuits, distinguish what powers or moves something from what receives the power or movement. Both roles must correspond to actual wiring or transport. Original examples should require interpreting both roles and preserve recoverable physical consequences. Avoid claiming a current machine state from a historical sentence without checking current indicators.
