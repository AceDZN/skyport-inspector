The source distinguishes subject relatives, object relatives, omitted object-relative words and non-defining clauses. An object relative can contain its own subject; a subject relative cannot lose the relative subject. Omission and extra-information forms have different reading expectations. Do not claim broad mastery from a single identifying-clause puzzle. Use observable physical relationships so the entire clause selects the correct cargo or control.
