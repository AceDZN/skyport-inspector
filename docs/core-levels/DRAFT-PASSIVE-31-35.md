## 31 · Which Supply? — causal source identifies the hatch

**Base:** “Use the hatch that is opened by the floor button. Keep the button pressed.”

**Contrast:** “Use the hatch that is opened by the light receiver. Keep the receiver lit.”

An enclosed dogleg storage room has a floor-button cargo hatch and an independently receiver-powered pedestrian hatch. The affected objects are the two hatches; the different actual causes are pressure and light. The floor button does not secretly power the emitter, so the two clauses cannot identify the same circuit. Cargo retrieval through a visible portal link maintains the pressure branch. A different valid portal configuration routes the beam for the receiver branch. One hatch leads through a low storage bay, the other to a raised observation landing reached by an ordinary lift.

**Ordinary chain:** inspect live wiring/response → obtain cargo or connect the optical pair → observe which real leaf moves → maintain the named source → physically reach its destination. Opening the other hatch permits a wrong-route visit without the requested unlock; return to the common foyer and configure the correct source. A clever equivalent pressure or light arrangement remains valid. Current pressure/receiver power must affect collision, not just a matching hatch label.

**Pending proof:** independent circuits, both true full-clearance openings, sustained supply, wrong branch/return and actual white sightlines. This narrowly practices reading the agent/source *by*-phrase; it does not assess sentence transformation.

## 32 · Carried or Powered? — two different causal roles

**Base:** “One cube is carried by the platform. Put that cube on RELEASE and keep POWER pressed.”

**Contrast:** “The platform is powered by the cube on POWER. Move that cube to SERVICE and use the lower door.”

A freight shaft contains one real loose cube travelling on a horizontal carrier and a second cube maintaining its POWER pressure. Base identifies the **carried object**; contrast identifies the **power source**. RELEASE plus POWER opens the upper freight route. SERVICE with POWER absent opens the lower pedestrian return. Both bodies are physically identical and interchangeable; final current conditions, not provenance, determine access.

**Ordinary chain:** watch actual carrier/cargo contact → collect travelling cargo at a dock → carry it through the return portal path → release it on RELEASE while the original supply remains pressed → use upper transport. Contrast removes the accessible source cube, physically braking the carrier, and powers SERVICE instead. Picking up the wrong cube first visibly stops the platform; replace it and wait for its smooth resumed cycle. No item vanishes or becomes unreachable when power is removed.

**Pending proof:** stable loose/held transport, real midrail brake/restart, current pressure and dock geometry, and safe retrieval after an early source removal. The phrase *by the platform* and *by the cube* must describe real opposite roles, not cargo colour names. Equivalent swaps achieving the same maintained conditions are accepted.

## 33 · What Is Blocked? — beam and cargo have different obstacles

**Base:** “The beam is blocked by the cube. Clear the beam and use the powered lift.”

**Contrast:** “The cube is blocked by the hatch. Open the hatch and take the cube to SERVICE.”

In a glass-sided light tunnel, one ordinary cargo body interrupts a real beam while a separate opaque access hatch prevents the player reaching it. A reachable service switch opens that hatch. Both observations are true in the same starting fixture: the cube blocks light, while the hatch blocks access to the cube. Clearing the cube lights the lift receiver. Putting it on SERVICE opens an independent lower cargo passage. There is no fictional “blocked” icon standing in for either solid.

**Ordinary chain:** operate the hatch → enter or use a physically fitting portal to reach cargo → pick up the actual cube → either park it clear of the ray and ride the newly powered lift, or deliver it to SERVICE and take that passage. E aimed at the wrong object cannot substitute for opening the physical barrier. Cargo can always be put down, regrasped and repositioned. If both routes become physically open, only the requested destination is credited; no hidden cargo-role check rejects a valid equivalent.

**Pending proof:** real ray occlusion, actual grab obstruction before opening, body-safe hatch behavior and cargo recovery from every permitted drop area. The sentences describe which thing receives which action; they do not imply that the cube is the hatch's power supply.

## 34 · A Process, Not a Person — timed shutters versus a retained hatch

**Base:** “The inspection shutters are opened by the timer. Wait for a clear passage and cross.”

**Contrast:** “The cargo hatch is released by TEST. Release it, switch TEST off and use its latched route.”

An enclosed stepped workshop has two serial inspection leaves under one real timer and a separate cargo hatch with a visible hold-open catch. TEST provides a real pulse to release the hatch. Its physical access remains after TEST is off; the inspection shutters continue to open/close according to their own live timer. The routes differ in waiting/planning rather than compass direction. Use one generous safe recess between serial leaves; do not create a forced crush sprint.

**Ordinary chain:** base follows actual current clearances through the timed gallery; a missed window waits safely. Contrast causes a genuine release, observes the catch, turns TEST off and moves cargo through the released access to a lower lift. Walking toward an old opening meets a currently solid leaf; repeating a real pulse or waiting restores access. A body pressing TEST or an equivalent optical pulse is valid if it really releases the same catch.

**Pending proof:** both timer clearances, intermediate refuge, actual latch persistence and current TEST-off behavior. An optional process note may say “Cargo is transferred here” without naming an actor; that omission cannot justify guessing who performed a prior delivery. No past-passive or hidden-agent mastery is claimed from this present-passive task.

## 35 · The Supply Loop — combine affected objects across two rooms

**Base:** “The lift is powered by the receiver, and ACCESS is opened by cargo pressure. Keep both supplies active and reach the control gallery.”

**Contrast:** “SERVICE is opened by the floor button, and the lift is stopped by loss of light. Keep the receiver dark and use SERVICE.”

A two-floor U-shaped plant separates a cargo-pressure access doorway from the receiver-powered lift beyond it. A second ordinary cube is retrievable through a remote white alcove. Base needs actual ACCESS pressure while an optical pair supplies the lift; the player must plan a return route that does not accidentally remove either supply. SERVICE pressure with receiver darkness opens a different lower loop. Both routes and two cargo bodies are fixed in advance; labels identify devices but do not invert actor/patient roles.

**Ordinary chain:** retrieve cargo → maintain ACCESS → configure a real receiver connection → board the live lift → reach its genuine dock/gallery. Contrast parks cargo on SERVICE, keeps light absent and walks the lower loop. Removing access pressure closes that actual doorway; carrying a cube into the beam brakes the real lift rather than merely reducing a score. Reposition cargo or restore the beam to recover; the ground loop remains reachable and no interruption-history penalty is added.

**Pending proof:** independent conjunction, source loss at both stages, body/cargo optical occlusion, lift disembark seams, wrong route and safe return. This is a transfer of two causal passive clauses, not five lookalike “is powered by” direction puzzles.
