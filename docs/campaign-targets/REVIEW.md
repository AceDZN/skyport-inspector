# Campaign target inspection and practical implementation review

These six generated outputs were visually inspected as they returned from built-in imagegen. They guide visual design for the single50-level campaign from `CAMPAIGN-50.md`. The prior grade-selector snapshot is historical; none of these targets depicts a grade menu or an answer-card interface.

## Shared identity to preserve

Warm ceramic/concrete planes with a small repeatable tile/wear vocabulary; dark graphite structural depth; aluminum hinges, rails and handled devices; sunlight/contact shadows; botanical glimpses that frame rather than obstruct the route. Violet A and amber B remain visually distinct, with explicit letters and shapes in actual runtime. The local original cargo module supplies a coherent hero silhouette. The guide can use the existing independently rigged clock-face automaton; generated robot variations do not require new models.

First-person normal working camera, compact reticle/mission/interaction hint and90-percent scene occupancy recur across targets. English text is short and LTR. There is no player hand/weapon rig, commercial-game branding, quiz overlay, document desk or giant HUD in the final set.

## Family-specific mechanical silhouettes

- **Gallery:** make the L turn real topology with shutter rails and a physical pad circuit. A second plausible pad prevents the only-device shortcut. Door progress must visibly respond to authoritative circuit state.
- **Lift/junction:** distinct floor heights, moving lift/rails and rotary bridge pivot matter more than decorative height. Show stable landing rims and a clear route before requiring motion precision.
- **Canal/workshop:** track/drive/roller forms distinguish conveyor motion from ferry motion. Real cargo transport, observable history and platform position must agree. Use safe recoverable routes; generated water richness does not substitute for collision/retry behavior.
- **Relay:** a thin continuous emitter/prism/receiver beam makes direction legible. Separate receiver alternatives must have locations matching the sentence. In target4 both receivers are generated above the door despite the requested above/beside contrast; runtime must correct that geometry.
- **Launch:** the final revision explicitly puts B high over void and the landing across open air. Preserve transformed speed/direction and a genuine flight gap; do not add a floor immediately outside B or count a corridor warp as a momentum puzzle.
- **Finale:** use a readable relay-to-lift-to-upper-bridge chain with an asymmetric bypass. Devices need stateful dependency, rather than a row of decorative props or invisible required-action flags.

## Generated limitations to avoid carrying into runtime

Some outputs omit A/B text, repeat B, show opaque glowing portal interiors, change the guide's face or facet the crate differently. These are recorded in per-family inspection files. Keep explicit runtime A/B labels, real linked views/traversal, the local original cargo and guide. Target6's glowing cargo diamond is art shorthand; do not encode the repeated correct answer by glowing identity.

The images' material wear, reflected light, foliage density and background architecture are richer than the current simple Three.js kit. Use them to set composition and readable mechanical form, not to claim image-equivalent rendering. A small set of reusable bevel/rail/hinge parts, shared materials and bounded foliage can approximate their hierarchy without new paid models. Maintain GPU texture/draw limits and lower-resolution portal fallback honestly.

## Verification boundary

All six final PNGs, exact prompts and inspection notes are saved locally; manifest records dimensions, byte counts and hashes. One launch candidate was rejected and retained with a documented reason. No runtime source or other game was modified by this target pass. No Tripo generation, rig, purchase or credit spend occurred.

After implementation, compare actual gameplay from each materially new family against its target: camera/space occupancy, route depth, module scale/contact, mechanical connection and consequence, text contrast, portal perspective and visible player action. Review captures across all50 actual rooms separately to detect repetition, dead space, clipping and unclear routes. Design targets and catalogue rows are never evidence that a level has been played or completed.
