## 41 · Together — two independent supplies

**Base:** “If both buttons are pressed, the freight gate opens. Keep both pressed and use that gate.”

**Contrast:** “If SERVICE is pressed and POWER is empty, the inspection gate opens. Arrange that supply and use inspection.”

**Physical assembly:** Two separated pressure platforms in an enclosed cargo cross-vault feed a real AND freight leaf. A separate service branch uses SERVICE with POWER absent. Two ordinary cargo bodies start in differently accessed bays, one retrieved through a portal and one reached by a low lift. No pad requires a particular object or weight category; both native cube masses remain equal unless a separate mass change is implemented/tested.

**Ordinary causal chain:** acquire independent supports → release them on both pads or SERVICE alone → see the corresponding actual leaf move → maintain the supplies and walk the chosen route. A transient player/held-body press is allowed but cannot replace an absent supply after the player leaves it. Removing one support physically changes the AND result. Wrong allocation closes the requested leaf or opens the other branch; regrasp/reallocate and return safely. Verify both current conjunctions, swapped cargo, source loss and approach-safe doors. This is a repeatable machine law, not a conditional truth-table quiz.

## 42 · An Arrival to Wait For — a future result is not a selected target

**Base:** “If you keep the gallery receiver lit, the lift will reach its upper dock. Wait for it, then use the gallery.”

**Contrast:** “If you keep the service receiver lit, the carrier will reach the service dock. Wait for it, then use service.”

**Physical assembly:** A split-height enclosed atrium has one vertical lift and one horizontal carrier powered by different real receivers. One emitter/portal pair can supply either destination; each has a stopped-dock leaf and a safe waiting landing. A selected beam route is only the start of an actual journey. A low catch and existing-style return stairs/lift preserve recovery from a miss.

**Ordinary causal chain:** align the real beam → observe actual receiver light → board/wait for physical motion → wait for true stopped support and clear dock → disembark to the named branch. A power cut brakes the machine at its actual pose; it does not fulfil the predicted arrival. Restore the beam and resume. Choosing the other receiver enables the other real machine and wrong destination, without an irreversible error. Verify both powered routes, interrupted phases and actual dock arrival; no `willReach` flag or instantaneous position change is permitted.

## 43 · If the Beam Is Blocked — a negative condition has a consequence

**Base:** “If the beam is blocked, the archive lift does not move. Clear it and use the lift.”

**Contrast:** “If the beam is blocked and SERVICE is pressed, the service gate opens. Keep that condition and use service.”

**Physical assembly:** A glass-sided light tunnel crosses the actual SERVICE plate. A cube parked there simultaneously blocks the ray and presses the pad. A separate parking shelf clears the optical path without asserting a fake “unblocked” label. Receiver light powers an archive lift; SERVICE pressure with darkness opens a real ground bypass. The lift and lower route are spatially distinct, not differently named compass gates.

**Ordinary causal chain:** observe the actual cargo/ray obstruction → either move cargo clear or place it on SERVICE → see current light/pressure change → use the resulting physical route. Taking cargo off SERVICE under contrast removes one condition even if the beam is still blocked by the player; darkness alone is insufficient. Restoring the cube or optical path recovers normally. Verify actual oriented-body occlusion, current conjunction and lift brake/resume. The task does not forbid every temporary interruption or remember a previous obstruction.

## 44 · If Not Docked, Wait — conditional action on current geometry

**Base:** “If both transfer decks are docked, the transfer gate opens. Wait for that opening and cross the freight route.”

**Contrast:** “If the inspection lift is at its lower dock, its door opens. Board it there and use inspection.”

**Physical assembly:** An enclosed transport hall has two familiar carriers in series with a broad safe refuge, plus a separate inspection lift. The freight leaf depends on actual stopped alignment of both relevant deck ends, never the chosen destinations or an old arrival event. Generous dwell overlap makes this a planning problem rather than a tiny timing target. The inspection route uses a different vertical boarding operation. Both exist under both missions.

**Ordinary causal chain:** observe/call actual machines → wait on a solid bank/refuge → transfer only after the leaf is genuinely clear and decks provide support → reach the correct bay. The alternate boards at the actual lower lift dock and rides to inspection. A missed interval waits for another; a real fall reaches a return route. ## 45 · Predict, Configure, Transfer — combine independent conditions

**Base:** “If cargo reaches STORE and the gallery receiver stays lit, the lift will carry you to the archive. Arrange both supplies, then board.”

**Contrast:** “If SERVICE is pressed and the gallery receiver stays dark, the inspection hatch will open. Arrange those conditions and use inspection.”

**Physical assembly:** A final enclosed U-shaped conditional workshop separates a remote storage plate from an optical lift supply. The one portal pair is needed for cargo transport around an opaque return bend and can later be configured for the receiver. A latched pedestrian access, physically released by a genuine pulse, permits this reconfiguration without trapping cargo. STORE plus light enables the archive lift; SERVICE plus darkness enables the lower return. No historical pulse requirement is added to the exit: the actual latch/access and current supplies are enough.

**Ordinary causal chain:** physically release access → deliver cargo through a fitting pair → configure/restore the required optical state → inspect both current supplies → board the enabled lift or use the lower opening. Cargo arrival alone does not make the future transport happen; light alone does not replace pressure. Correct an interrupted connection or wrong pad by ordinary placement/regrasping; phase resumes physically. Equivalent clever routes and cargo assignments stay valid. Verify the entire route, rather than assuming41's conjunction or42's powered lift automatically proves this new assembly.
