## Source grounding and limits

Exact already-read sources include G7 mixed-tenses, spatial prepositions, imperatives and practical texts; G8 mixed account, relative clause, reference and practical sources; G9 contextual present perfect/past simple, reported speech and practical sources. Each row lists its exact canonical URLs, so selected coverage can be audited without claiming whole chapters.

Additional exact modules were discovered/fetched/read before the relevant rows:

- [G8 connectors and time clauses](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-8/chapter-11/connectors-and-time-clauses): before/after/when/while express actual temporal relationships; their words alone do not select every verb tense.
- [G8 present passive](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-8/chapter-10/present-simple-passive-reference): subject receives an action; by identifies an agent when named. A participle alone does not make a sentence past.
- [G9 zero/first conditionals](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-9/chapter-11/conditionals-reference): ordinary repeated machine law versus a real future possibility, with present if-clause and present/will/modal/imperative result as appropriate. Do not extend a simplified teaching rule into “English can never use will after if”; willingness and other uses are outside this pack.
- [G8 comparisons](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-8/chapter-11/adjectives-and-comparison): compare the stated property and a defined pair/group. Higher/larger does not identify an unrelated property.

Current status is always explicit. A dated repair/inspection does not itself prove present damage, expiry or clearance. Both current-source routes in34 remain physically possible, making the reading matter. A route that is the only physically usable one would demonstrate compliance, not independently prove tense comprehension.

The game practices selected functional reading, reference and action planning. It does not assess free grammar production, extended prose, all tense forms, all relative/reported constructions, speaking/listening proficiency, pronunciation or retained mastery. Automated real-input runs establish feasibility and causal behavior, not fun or learning gains.
