# Concrete upper campaign fixtures31–50

Status: **authored and preflight checked; no level in this handoff is claimed completed through normal public inputs**. The numbered data override is `src/data/worlds-upper.json`. `docs/campaign-art/author-upper-worlds.py` is its reproducible, explicitly authored source. It reads no mission, goal or catalogue. The runtime loader selects a fixed fixture by number; changing the English keeps every object, history, machine, surface and position identical.

The twenty fixtures use relay gardens, launch wells, history depots, width-constrained canal routes, dual vaults, multi-stop shafts, L galleries, return conveyors, conveyor junctions, ferry canals and combined finales. They do not rotate a common court. Machinery geometry/collision is supplied by the engine; existing original cargo and guide assets are reused. There was no asset purchase or generation for these fixtures.

## Reading the route anchors

Every fixture has `routePoints` for its actual cargo, surfaces, pads, exits and controls. Coordinates are world XYZ. Cargo/pad/device anchors describe the object, not a player teleport location. `control:ID` is the exact target to face for E. Lift controls ride with the platform; `control:ID:base|middle|upper|top` gives that control at each stop. Junction controls stay on the fixed safe hub at pivot+[-1,.9,2]. These are guidance for the real-input tester; assigning player/body positions is not play evidence.

All wall panels use eligible, axis-aligned, backed surfaces. Ordinary wall portal placement centers .25m below the surface's nominal Y. Floor wells are actual3.5×3.5m holes, with separate bottom hosts3m below the start floor. A floor portal transforms the falling velocity into the horizontal velocity needed to cross the5m gap to the high landing. There is no `launchUsed` completion requirement. Room49 has two actual wells, two distinct heights and an opaque front skirt wide enough to hide the second pair from the lower start. Its first wall B is factory preset so the first pair does not require a lower sightline through that skirt.

## Static preflight evidence and limits

`npx tsx --test --test-reporter=spec tests/campaign-conformance.test.ts` output is retained in `UPPER_CONFORMANCE.log`. This checks references, catalogue fidelity, actual initial prohibited state31, fixed replay events43/48, latch semantics, power circuits, contrast fixture equality and synthetic objective distinctions. It is not a public-input run.

`npx tsx docs/campaign-art/audit-upper-fixtures.ts` writes `UPPER_STATIC_PROBE.json`. No relay path intersects a permanent authored wall before its receiver. All initial relays are either physically screened or genuinely disabled by a pad circuit. The49 middle receiver starts screened. The43 replay crate-b drops atX1.1 and remains grounded on the moving rack's upper deck;48's diamond is actually delivered onto its plinth. Initial cargo body support/penetration was additionally checked against solid and platform AABBs: no initial unsupported cargo or penetrating body was found.

Inventory positions are independently **5first /5second /5third**. Only7/15 are the nearest initial object. These counts use actual cargo XYZ, not array shuffling alone. Owner/history/belt roles stay fixed; multi-pad37/50 and non-identity tasks are excluded from that sample.
