# First target inspection

The first generated target is rejected for mechanical orientation and prop style. It shows the amber launch aperture on a wall nearly coplanar with the destination galleries, so its visible outward normal does not supply the required across-room trajectory. Front rail segments also appear across landing paths, and the oversized weapon differs from the actual compact original split-coil device. These are planning-image defects, not native game defects. The two substantial shafts, readable height difference, warm landing lights and enclosed crossbeam remain useful architectural reference. A corrected target must put the launch aperture on a genuine opposite wall facing the landings and preserve unobstructed arrival paths.

The selected second target, `twin-shaft-target-v2.png`, corrects the launch wall to the left opposite the two right landings, opens their arrival edges and uses the actual compact original device as a prop reference. It is useful for warm edge lighting, twin-shaft architecture, two clearly named heights and restrained HUD composition. It still does not establish metric geometry, collision clearance, a trajectory, or a playable approach to every drop: the floor aperture's perspective is illustrative, and its runtime normal must be exactly the solid floor normal. The final foreground approach and every remaining rail need actual capsule clearance. Exact launch height, span, depth and native landing evidence must govern construction. Do not copy picture coordinates into physics or claim this raster as a working room.
